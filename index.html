Mexico

Mexico has a holiday called Day of the Dead (Dia de los Muertos) which is celebrated on Nov 1-2.

On this day people usually leave out offerings for the dead. Either on the person's grave or on their ofrenda.

Dia de los Muertos is split into two days.

Nov 1st is known as "Dia de los Angelitos", which is dedicated to honoring infants and children who passed away

Nov 2nd is known as "Dia de los Muertos", which is dedicated to honoring the adults who passed away.
